Our favourites

There's a lot to do here. These are the things we keep coming back to.

Henningsvær

Walk around the fishing village and pick up a cinnamon bun at Lysstøperiet. If you're staying a few days, have a burger at Klatrekafeen and dinner at Trevarefabrikken. Both are worth it.

Surfing at Unstad

Easy to do even if you don't bring your own gear. You can rent everything you need nearby.

A short hike close by

There's a short walk from the lodge that's well worth the time.

Dinner at Ure

A proper food experience, and one of our favourite evenings out.

Reine and Bunes Beach

A full day out if you're up for a drive. Take the ferry from Reine over to Bunes, and spend time on the beach. Just remember to catch the ferry back.

Skiing in winter

Conditions change a lot from day to day. We'd recommend booking a local guide who knows where the best snow and the best mountains are.
