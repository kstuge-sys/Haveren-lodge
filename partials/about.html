The lodge

Storøya Lodge was drawn by Tord Kvien at Niels Torp Architects, and it's new. The shape is inspired by the Vikings who lived on these islands a thousand years ago. It's built to handle the weather and to look after the people coming in from it.

The island has a history of its own. A fisherman once lived here and kept a small farm to get by. The red barn and boathouse are still part of the place.

Architecture — Tord Kvien, Niels Torp Architects
