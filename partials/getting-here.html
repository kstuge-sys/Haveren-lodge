Getting here

By air. Most guests fly into Leknes or Svolvær.

From Leknes Airport, it's about 30 minutes by car. From Svolvær Airport, it's about 1 hour. In both cases you follow the E10 to the turn-off towards Kvalnes.

If you'd rather fly to the mainland, Harstad/Narvik Airport, Evenes also works. It's a longer drive, around 3 hours, but a lovely one, and it can be a good way in if the flights are better.

By boat. You can also come by sea from Bodø to Svolvær. From Svolvær, the route is the same as from the airport.

The last bit. From the E10, turn off towards Kvalnes. Shortly after, you take a small gravel road. It's narrow, with some holes and a few turns, so please drive slowly. It takes about five minutes.

As you get close to the island, a small pier appears and leads you across to Storøya. A red barn comes into view almost straight away with a boathouse by the water. You've arrived.

A car helps. Lofoten is easiest to explore by car, so we'd recommend booking a rental ahead of time, especially in summer and the ski season.
