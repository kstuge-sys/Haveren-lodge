Welcome to Storøya

Storøya Lodge sits on the waterfront, facing north to the open sea. Tall mountains surround it, and on the horizon stands Hoven, a lone peak you’ll recognise straight away.

The lodge is just off Vestvågøy, in the middle of the Lofoten archipelago, and easy to reach by car.

Come for the surf, the skiing, the hiking or the northern lights. Or come to sit by the window with a coffee and watch the weather change. Most people end up doing a bit of both.
