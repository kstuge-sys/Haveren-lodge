Welcome

Storøya Lodge sits by the water, just off Vestvågøy in Lofoten, with a red barn and a boathouse nearby. The location is central, so there's plenty to do close by, and a calm, peaceful place to come back to afterwards.

Come for the surf, the skiing, the hiking or the northern lights. Or come to sit by the window with a coffee and watch the weather change. Most people end up doing a bit of both.
