The lodge

Storøya Lodge is inspired by the Iron Age boathouses: long buildings built to face the sea. On Vestvågøy, 62 of Northern Norway’s 82 known Viking boathouses were found, so it felt like the right place for the idea.

The old farms here were built around a closed square yard. We turned that around and opened it up again, towards the Atlantic. The lodge is new, and it’s built to handle the weather and to look after the people coming in from it.

Inside there are big windows, a fire, and a lot of calm. It’s a good place to come back to after a long day outside.

The island has a history of its own. A fisherman once lived here and kept a small farm to get by. The red barn and boathouse are still part of the place.
